# As a ***Web Manager***, What Are you Being Asked to Do?

As a web manager, you are responsible for regularly posting news and events and
making sure pages are up to date.

## Daily

There's no need to wait until the end of the week or month to tell your
community what's happening and give them timely information. Let them know about
a flu shot clinic, updates to the process of reporting bullying or remind them
of the drop-off procedures or anything else at any time by:

- [Adding news](../creating-and-editing-news-messages.md)
- [Adding events](../creating-and-editing-events.md)

## Monthly

You should regularly edit the
[Homepage Highlights](../managing-homepage-highlights.md) to make sure they contain
the most important and relevant information. Some ideas:

### Ongoing/periodically:
- Testing dates and information (i.e. MAP, MCAP, etc.)
- Instructional seminar offerings
- Report card dates and information on how to access and interpret them
- Health services info (i.e. what to do if your child needs to take medication at school)
- Concerts and performances
- Student and staff awards/recognitions

### Fall

- School supply lists
- Registration
- Drop-off procedures
- Updating Family File
- Back to school nights/new student orientations/open houses
- How to report absences, late arrivals, early dismissals
- Homecoming
- Picture Day
- Parent/teacher conferences
- Sports tryout schedules
- Parking permit applications

### Winter

- Information on making sure parents and students are receiving texts (so they
  know about snow days)
- Where to get assistance with winter clothing (or how to contribute to clothing
  drives)
- Any winter break messaging
- Course registration
- Sports tryout schedules
- Graduation information
- Hour of Code

### Spring

- Spring break and summer break information
- How to find meal programs in the summer
- Summer reading lists
- Sports tryout schedules
- Picture Day
- Simulated Congressional Hearings
- Battle of the Books

## Yearly

At the beginning of the school year, you should go through all the pages in the
main menu and make sure they are up to date. Pay special attention to the
[Staff List](../creating-and-managing-staff-lists.md) and
[About page](../advanced-pages.md).
