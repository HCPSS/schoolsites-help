# Logging In

Log into your school's website by going to your school's website and adding
"/login" to the end of the website URL (i.e. "http://waves.hcpss.org/login").

![A screebshot of a browser with the address set to https://wlhs.hcpss.org/login](images/login-url.png)

You will be redirected to the HCPSS login page. Enter your HCPSS credentials.

![The HCPSS login page with arrows pointing to the username and password fields](images/hcpss-login.png)

You will know you’re logged in when the black and grey tool bar appears at the
top of your screen.

![A screenshot of the Wilde Lake High School website with an arrow pointing to the toolbar at the top of the page](images/toolbar.png)

To log out, click your username and then "log out" in the toolbar.

![A screenshot of the Wilde Lake High School website with arrows pointing to the username and logout link in the toolbar](images/logout.png)
